Shift-click or right-click always erases; drag to keep erasing. Activity is the change per 100 steps, relative to the world's mass.

What you're looking at. Each world is a continuous cellular automaton in the Lenia family, using a mass-conserving update (MaCE): mass is never created or destroyed, only moved toward where the rule's growth field attracts it. The three colours are three interacting channels; the world wraps around at its edges.

Where the worlds come from. Each rule was found by an evolutionary search that judged worlds at step 2,700. Run them far past that: some keep moving indefinitely, some merge into one body, some freeze.
